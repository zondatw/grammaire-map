The subjunctive is a mood (not a tense) used in subordinate clauses after expressions of **doubt, emotion, necessity, or desire**.

**Common triggers:**
- *il faut que* (it is necessary that)
- *je veux que* (I want that)
- *je suis content(e) que* (I am happy that)
- *bien que* (although)

**Formation:** Take the *ils/elles* present stem + subjunctive endings:

| Person | Ending |
|--------|--------|
| je | -e |
| tu | -es |
| il/elle | -e |
| nous | -ions |
| vous | -iez |
| ils/elles | -ent |

Example — *parler* (ils parlent → stem: parl-):
*que je parle, que tu parles, qu'il parle…*

**Irregular stems:** *être (soi-/som-)*, *avoir (ai-/ay-)*, *aller (aill-/all-)*, *faire (fass-/fas-)*

## Examples

- *Il faut que tu parles plus fort.* (You need to speak louder.)
- *Je suis content qu'elle soit là.* (I'm glad she's here.)

Spanish has four definite articles that correspond to English "the." They agree in **gender** (masculine/feminine) and **number** (singular/plural) with the noun they modify.

|---|---|---|

- Nouns ending in **-o** are usually **masculine**: *el libro*, *el gato*, *el vino*

Most Spanish nouns form their plural by adding **-s** (vowel ending) or **-es** (consonant ending). Several patterns produce spelling or accent changes.

**1. Nouns ending in -z → change z to c, add -es:**
- *pez → peces* (fish)
- *vez → veces* (time/instance)
- *lápiz → lápices* (pencil)
- *voz → voces* (voice)

**2. Accent mark changes:**
- Nouns with a written accent on the last syllable typically drop the accent in the plural: *lección → lecciones*, *camión → camiones*, *francés → franceses*
- Nouns ending in -n or -s that are unstressed in the singular gain an accent in the plural to maintain stress: *joven → jóvenes*, *orden → órdenes*, *examen → exámenes*

- *el lunes → los lunes* (Monday/Mondays)
- *el virus → los virus*
- *la crisis → las crisis*

**4. Compound nouns:** Generally only the first element (or no element) changes: *el pasaporte → los pasaportes*.

The **present subjunctive** is used in subordinate clauses triggered by certain expressions. It is formed by taking the **yo present indicative**, dropping the **-o**, and adding the "opposite" vowel endings.

**Formation:**
- **-ar verbs** → take yo form, drop -o, add **-e endings**: *hablar → hablo → habl- → hable, hables, hable, hablemos, habléis, hablen*
- **-er / -ir verbs** → take yo form, drop -o, add **-a endings**: *comer → como → com- → coma, comas, coma, comamos, comáis, coman*

This means irregular yo forms carry into the subjunctive: *tener → tengo → tenga*, *hacer → hago → haga*, *conocer → conozco → conozca*.

| Impersonal | es importante que, es necesario que | *Es necesario que estudies.* |
| Recommendations | recomendar que, aconsejar que | *Te recomiendo que lo hagas.* |
| Doubt/Denial | dudar que, no creer que | *Dudo que sepa la respuesta.* |
| Ojalá | ojalá (+ subjunctive) | *Ojalá llueva mañana.* |
