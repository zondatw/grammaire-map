## Explanation

The **present subjunctive** is used in subordinate clauses triggered by certain expressions. It is formed by taking the **yo present indicative**, dropping the **-o**, and adding the "opposite" vowel endings.

**Formation:**
- **-ar verbs** → take yo form, drop -o, add **-e endings**: *hablar → hablo → habl- → hable, hables, hable, hablemos, habléis, hablen*
- **-er / -ir verbs** → take yo form, drop -o, add **-a endings**: *comer → como → com- → coma, comas, coma, comamos, comáis, coman*

This means irregular yo forms carry into the subjunctive: *tener → tengo → tenga*, *hacer → hago → haga*, *conocer → conozco → conozca*.

**Common triggers (WEIRDO categories):**

| Category | Trigger example | Example sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Wishes | querer que, desear que | *Quiero que vengas.* (I want you to come.) |
| Emotion | alegrarse de que, temer que | *Me alegra que estés aquí.* |
| Impersonal | es importante que, es necesario que | *Es necesario que estudies.* |
| Recommendations | recomendar que, aconsejar que | *Te recomiendo que lo hagas.* |
| Doubt/Denial | dudar que, no creer que | *Dudo que sepa la respuesta.* |
| Ojalá | ojalá (+ subjunctive) | *Ojalá llueva mañana.* |

**Key rule:** The subjunctive requires a **change of subject** between the main and subordinate clause. If the subject is the same, use the infinitive: *Quiero ir* (same subject) vs. *Quiero que tú vayas* (different subjects).

## Examples

| Spanish | English |
|---------|---------|
| Quiero que hables más despacio. | I want you to speak more slowly. |
| Es importante que comamos bien. | It is important that we eat well. |
| No creo que tenga razón. | I don't think he's right. |
| Espero que vengas a la fiesta. | I hope you come to the party. |
| Ojalá haga buen tiempo. | I hope the weather is nice. |
| Te aconsejo que descanses. | I advise you to rest. |

## Pitfall

The most common error is using the indicative after a subjunctive trigger: saying *"Quiero que vienes"* is wrong — it must be *"Quiero que vengas."* Also remember: same subject = infinitive (*Quiero comer*), different subject = subjunctive (*Quiero que ella coma*).
