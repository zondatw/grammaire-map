## Explanation

The **preterite** (pretérito indefinido) expresses completed actions in the past with a clear beginning and/or end.

**Regular -ar verbs** (e.g., *hablar*):
| yo | tú | él/ella | nosotros | vosotros | ellos |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hablé | hablaste | habló | hablamos | hablasteis | hablaron |

**Regular -er and -ir verbs** (e.g., *comer*, *vivir*):
| yo | tú | él/ella | nosotros | vosotros | ellos |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| comí | comiste | comió | comimos | comisteis | comieron |
| viví | viviste | vivió | vivimos | vivisteis | vivieron |

Note: -er and -ir verbs share identical preterite endings.

**When to use the preterite:**
- Completed past actions: *Ayer comí pizza.* (Yesterday I ate pizza.)
- Actions with a specific time frame: *Vivió en Madrid tres años.* (She lived in Madrid for three years.)
- A sequence of completed past events: *Llegué, me senté y empecé a leer.* (I arrived, sat down, and started to read.)
- Interrupting actions (paired with imperfect): *Cuando llegué, ella dormía.* (When I arrived, she was sleeping.)

**Common time markers:** *ayer*, *anteayer*, *el lunes pasado*, *hace dos días*, *en 2020*, *de repente*, *entonces*.

## Examples

| Spanish | English |
|---------|---------|
| Hablé con ella ayer. | I spoke with her yesterday. |
| ¿Comiste? | Did you eat? |
| Vivimos en París el año pasado. | We lived in Paris last year. |
| Llegaron tarde. | They arrived late. |
| Estudié toda la noche. | I studied all night. |
| ¿A qué hora saliste? | What time did you leave? |

## Pitfall

The preterite *nosotros* form for -ar verbs (*hablamos*) is identical to the present tense form — context determines meaning. Also, be careful with the accent marks: *hablé* and *habló* (with accent) are preterite; *hablo* (no accent) is present tense.
