## Explanation

Relative pronouns connect a noun (the antecedent) to a relative clause that describes it.

**che** — the most common relative pronoun. Used for both people and things. Serves as either the **subject** or the **direct object** of the relative clause. Never used after a preposition.
- *Il libro che leggo è interessante.* (The book that I'm reading is interesting.) — direct object
- *La ragazza che abita qui si chiama Sofia.* (The girl who lives here is called Sofia.) — subject

**cui** — used after **prepositions** (a, di, con, per, su, da, in, etc.) for both people and things.
- *La persona a cui ho scritto è mio zio.* (The person to whom I wrote is my uncle.)
- *Il motivo per cui sono venuto.* (The reason why I came.)
- *Il libro di cui parli.* (The book you're talking about.)

**il quale / la quale / i quali / le quali** — formal alternative to *che* and *cui*. Useful after prepositions to avoid ambiguity about gender/number:
- *La figlia del direttore, la quale è molto brava...* (The director's daughter, who is very good...)
- After prepositions: *al quale*, *della quale*, *con i quali*, etc.

**chi** — "whoever" or "those who." Not preceded by a noun antecedent. Often used in proverbs:
- *Chi dorme non piglia pesci.* (The early bird catches the worm. / Literally: Those who sleep don't catch fish.)
- *Non so chi sia.* (I don't know who it is.)

## Examples

| Italian | English |
|---------|---------|
| Il film che abbiamo visto era noioso. | The film that we saw was boring. |
| L'amico con cui viaggio si chiama Luca. | The friend I'm traveling with is called Luca. |
| La città in cui sono nato è Napoli. | The city in which I was born is Naples. |
| Chi studia ottiene buoni risultati. | Those who study get good results. |
| Il professore del quale ti ho parlato. | The professor I told you about. |

## Pitfall

Never use **che** after a preposition. *La persona con che parlo* is wrong — use *La persona con cui parlo*. Similarly, do not omit **che** as English sometimes omits "that": *Il libro che leggo* (not *il libro leggo*).
